Privacy Policy

BoopStash collects no data. There is no BoopStash server, and nothing you put in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your devices

  • Your notes, images and settings are stored on your device, inside the app's sandboxed container.
  • There are no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no update checks. The app has no network access of its own.
  • Titles, text recognition in images and any other AI features use Apple's on-device frameworks only. Nothing is sent to a cloud model.
  • The Markdown preview loads nothing from the internet. Remote images are named, not fetched.
  • Scripts and formatters run in a separate sandboxed process with no network and no file access.

The clipboard

BoopStash never watches your clipboard. It reads it only when you act: pasting, choosing a capture command, using Services or dropping something on the window. Items that password managers mark as concealed or transient are never stored.

iCloud sync

Sync through iCloud is planned. It will be optional and off by default. When it is on, your notes go only to the private database of BoopStash's iCloud container under your own Apple Account, which the developer cannot read. Every field the app stores is encrypted on your device before upload. With Advanced Data Protection turned on, that encryption is end to end and only your devices hold the keys. Without it, iCloud uses keys Apple can access, as it does for most iCloud data.

Files

Beyond its own container, the app can write only to a folder you choose, which is how export works.

Children

BoopStash collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app's source repository, where every change is visible. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
