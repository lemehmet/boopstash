BoopStash is a scratch pad that remembers. Paste a snippet, an agent's output or a screenshot, and find it again next week by what it was about, when you copied it, or where it came from.

KEPT AND FOUND
• Every note is kept and titled for you on your device.
• Search titles, text and the words in your screenshots, and narrow by age: today, this week, the last 30 days.
• Search by meaning too: "invoice" finds "bill for March", with a small language model on your Mac.
• Capture from anywhere with a shortcut or the Services menu, or from the terminal: some-command | boopstash.

AN EDITOR THAT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY
• No auto-closing quotes and no smart punctuation. Tab indents.
• VS Code or JetBrains key bindings.
• Syntax highlighting for the common languages, light and dark themes.
• Format Document for JSON, XML, JavaScript, TypeScript, CSS, HTML, YAML, Markdown, SQL, Python, Go, shell, C, C++ and Java.
• Fold code blocks, elements and Markdown sections.
• Boop's scripts: encode, decode, hash, convert, sort and more, on the selection or the whole note. Add your own.
• Markdown preview with the note's images.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no BoopStash server, no analytics.
• Optional iCloud sync to your own iCloud, with every field encrypted on your device. With Advanced Data Protection, end to end.
• Text recognition, titles and search by meaning run on your device.
• The clipboard is read only when you paste or capture.
• Export every note as Markdown files with their images.
