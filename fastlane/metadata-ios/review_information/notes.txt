PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
BoopStash is a private scratch pad for developers and other people who work with text: code snippets, terminal and AI-agent output, logs, JSON, screenshots. On iPhone and iPad it keeps those notes at hand: listed newest first, titled automatically, searchable by words, by meaning and by date, and editable in a plain-text editor with syntax highlighting. It is the companion of BoopStash for Mac but works on its own.

SETTING UP AND USING THE APP
No login, account or sample files are needed. On first launch the note list is empty.
1. Tap New Note and type, or tap From Clipboard to save copied text. The note is saved and titled automatically. (To save an image, use the Share sheet, step 2.)
2. From another app, use the Share sheet ▸ BoopStash to save text or an image as a note.
3. Search: pull down the list to reveal the Filter field; matches come from titles, text and text in images. The filter menu at the top left narrows by date (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days) and by label. Search by meaning: "invoice" also finds "bill for March". Long-press a note ▸ Show Similar Notes.
4. Open a note to read or edit it; code is syntax highlighted, and Markdown has a Preview.
5. Settings (gear button): the optional "Sync with iCloud" toggle (off by default) shares notes, and their labels, with BoopStash on the user's Mac through their own iCloud.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple iCloud (CloudKit, the user's own private database), only if the user turns on Sync with iCloud. It is off by default.
No other network service is used: no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no payment processor and no server of ours. All AI runs on the device: Apple's Vision framework for text in images, and a small embedding model bundled in the app (Core ML) for search by meaning. Titles come from a built-in rule-based titler; the iOS app does not use Apple Intelligence or Apple's Foundation Models framework.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions and on all supported devices: it does not depend on Apple Intelligence or on any regional service.
