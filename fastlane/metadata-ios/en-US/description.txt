BoopStash is a scratch pad for people who work with text: code snippets, terminal and AI-agent output, logs, JSON and screenshots. Save it in a second, and find it again next week by what it was about, even if you don't remember the words.

FIND IT BY MEANING
• Search by meaning with a small language model that runs on your device: "invoice" finds "bill for March".
• Show Similar Notes lists the notes closest to the one you're holding.
• Text inside your screenshots is recognised on the device and found by search.
• Narrow by age (today, the last 7 days, the last 30 days) or by label.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
• Share ▸ BoopStash saves text or an image from any app.
• From Clipboard saves what you just copied, only when you tap it.
• Every note is titled for you and listed newest first.

AN EDITOR THAT DOESN'T BREAK YOUR CODE
• No smart quotes, no smart dashes, no autocorrect: JSON and commands stay exactly as you pasted them.
• Syntax highlighting for the common languages, and a Markdown preview.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no BoopStash server, no analytics.
• Search by meaning and text recognition run on your device.
• Optional sync with BoopStash for Mac through your own iCloud, off until you turn it on.
