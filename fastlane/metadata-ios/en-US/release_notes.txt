• Paste an image into a note, or make a note of it with From Clipboard. Select its reference in the editor for Copy Text from Image and Insert Text Below.
• Settings ▸ Help shows what the app does and how it treats your notes, without going online.
• What's New: after an update, BoopStash shows once what changed. The notes stay in Settings ▸ What's New, and can be copied or saved as a note.
• Touch and hold an image in a note's preview to copy the text in it.
• Text read from images keeps its rows, so a screenshot of a table pastes into a spreadsheet as a table.
• With iCloud sync on, an edit made soon after a note first synced could end up in a "(conflict)" copy; it now stays in the note.
• The note list no longer stays on an old filter when the filter changes twice in quick succession.
• With the preview on, every note you open renders, not just the first.
• Labels made on your Mac show as coloured chips, and the Filter menu lists only the notes carrying the labels you check.
• Search by meaning: a search also finds notes that say the same thing in other words, using a small language model on your device. Show Similar Notes lists a note's closest neighbours.
• Notes shared from other apps are found by meaning too.
